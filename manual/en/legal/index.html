3. Privacy notice (what we collect)

We do not collect your personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-up forms, no online payments, no advertising, and no Google Analytics or other trackers. We do not sell or share personal data.

  • Hosting: this site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub automatically logs server data (for example IP address, device and browser details, date/time, pages viewed) — see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We cannot identify individuals from those logs.
  • Flash station page: that page loads the esptool-js library from the jsDelivr CDN, so your IP address reaches jsDelivr — see the jsDelivr privacy policy.
  • USB flashing: everything happens directly between your browser and your device (Web Serial). Nothing is uploaded to any server.
  • If you contact us: if you post in GitHub Issues or give us personal data at a market (for example an email address), we use it only to reply and to provide support, in line with the Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance. Providing it is voluntary; we do not pass it to third parties for marketing.

4. Cookies and local storage

  • This site sets no cookies at all, so there is nothing to consent to and no cookie banner.
  • If you manually switch on dark mode, we remember the theme preference in your browser's localStorage (on your device only, never sent to us, deleted when you clear browser data).
  • Nothing else is stored on your device.

5. Your rights (Hong Kong PDPO)

If you have given us personal data, you may request access, correction, or that we stop using it, by contacting us through GitHub Issues below. If you are in the EEA or another region with similar laws, you may also request access, erasure, or restriction of processing.

7. Accessibility

We aim at WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard operable, a skip-to-content link, 4.5:1 text contrast, dark mode and scalable text. If anything blocks you, please open a GitHub issue and we will fix it.
